## 2. Cell vocabulary

| Cell | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PASS` | observed to work, with an evidence link |
| `FAIL` | observed not to work, with the observed behaviour and an evidence link |
| `N/A` | the app does not expose that capability at all — a legitimate outcome, not a defect |
| `UNTESTED` | never measured. The only honest default. |

A cell may only become `PASS` from a recorded test run, and each run's evidence lives in
`docs/evidence/<date>/TC-E2E-02/<app>/`.

## 3. How capabilities become HUD buttons

The HUD enables a control only when the phone reports that the active session supports
the corresponding action (§10.1): the capability bitmask is derived from the session's
`PlaybackState` actions, not from a hard-coded list of known apps. Consequences that are
by design:

* an app with no seek support shows Seek as disabled with an explanation, rather than a
  button that silently does nothing (Rule 4);
* Next/Prev require `ACTION_SKIP_TO_NEXT/PREVIOUS`;
* the tap-to-seek zone on the progress bar only exists when `ACTION_SEEK_TO` is
  supported and the bar's touch zone can be at least 48 px tall (§10.2/DEC-29).

## 4. What the registry cannot claim

A row for an app version tested on one device does not extend to another version, and
"works for Spotify" is not a statement about media control in general. The registry is
per-app-version evidence; anything else is listed as `UNTESTED`.
